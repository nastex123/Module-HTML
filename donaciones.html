Why should you donate?

Your donation is not just a number, it is an act of will that transcends the material. In a world where noise and haste envelop us, donating is a reminder that empathy still exists.

Each contribution, no matter how small, represents the spark that ignites projects, ideas, and dreams. You don't donate out of charity, you donate because you believe in a future where knowledge and creativity are accessible to all.

As Seneca said: "It is not that we have little time, but that we lose much". Your time and resources, placed in our hands, become tools to build, not to consume.

What do we do with the money?

Transparency is our pact. Every peso donated goes to:

  • Site maintenance - Servers, domains, and security.
  • Development of free projects - Open tools for everyone.
  • Care of our pets - Food, veterinary care, and animal welfare.
  • Education and outreach - Philosophical and technical content without barriers.

We do not keep the money. Money is movement, it is flow. We receive it and transform it into immediate action.

How do we help with your donation?

When you donate, you not only support a website. You become part of a network that:

  • Plants seeds of critical thinking - Through reflective content.
  • Builds free tools - Open source for everyone.
  • Rescues and protects animals - Our pets are family.
  • Shares knowledge at no cost - Because information should be free.

We don't change the world alone. But together, donation after donation, we light a flame that dispels the darkness of indifference.
